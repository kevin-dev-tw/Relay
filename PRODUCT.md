# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React + GSAP (ScrollSmoother, ScrollTrigger). Tooling delegated to agent (Vite; styling is authored CSS with custom properties; fonts self-hosted via Fontsource). Fictional product: all claims, metrics, and customer names are illustrative and may be replaced by the owner.

## Users

Product and design team members — the people who will actually operate the tool day to day. They care about how the product feels in the hand and whether it fits their workflow, and they evaluate landing pages with a designer's eye.

## Product Purpose

An AI customer-support automation product (fictional). It resolves the bulk of repetitive support tickets automatically so human agents handle only the conversations that need judgment. Success on the landing page: the visitor believes the automation is trustworthy and requests access / starts a trial.

## Positioning

The automation is presented as a resolution engine, not a chatbot — the pitch is about tickets closed end-to-end, not deflection or FAQ matching.

## Operating Context

B2B evaluation scene: visitor arrives via referral or search, scans fast, compares against incumbent support tooling, needs enough substance to justify a demo or trial signup.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- AI resolves routine tickets across channels (chat, email); escalates with context to humans.
- All numbers, logos, and testimonials on the page are synthetic placeholders for a fictional product.

## Brand Commitments

User-pinned direction: 大膽前衛 — bold, avant-garde, cinematic scroll storytelling; award-tier ambition is the bar.

## Evidence on Hand

None — fictional product. No real customers, benchmarks, or press exist; the page may not cite real companies as customers.

## Product Principles

- Lead with resolution, not deflection.
- Show the machine working, not just marketing claims — the scroll itself should feel like the product.
- Craft is the proof: a janky or generic page would contradict the product's promise.
